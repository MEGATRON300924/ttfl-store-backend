# Vendor Legal Verification — Cross-Marketplace Plan

## Request
Implement a shared legal/business verification workflow across TTFL Store, TTFL Store Cars, and TTFL Store Homes.

## Product rules
- Registered Store: default badge for a newly registered/default store after platform registration checks; it does not mean legal identity has been verified.
- Verified Store: granted only after TTFL Store / The Tron Forge Limited completes and approves legal verification.
- Business Store: automatically granted together with Verified Store when legal/business verification is approved.
- Enterprise and Platinum remain existing commercial/status badges and must not be confused with legal verification.
- Rejected, withdrawn, or revoked legal verification must not leave a misleading Verified Store or Business Store badge.
- Existing vendor approval for marketplace access remains distinct from legal verification approval.

## Planned workflow
1. Vendor dashboard page: “Verify Store” with legal entity type, legal name, registration details where applicable, representative details, contact/address information, document uploads, declarations, and submission status.
2. Backend: authenticated vendor submission/read endpoints; private document storage/references; validation; audit trail; admin review queue/details; approve/reject with reason; controlled badge transitions.
3. Admin: dedicated legal verification review page/section, view submitted information/documents, approve or reject, and record reviewer/time/reason.
4. Public: show Registered Store by default; only show Verified Store + Business Store after legal approval.
5. Cars and Homes: expose the same verification state and vendor workflow using their existing account/store context, backed by the shared TTFL backend where possible.

## Security and data handling
- Legal documents must not be exposed through public store APIs or public static URLs.
- Admin-only document access; vendor can view own submission/status.
- Validate MIME type, size, and allowed document categories server-side.
- Do not mark a store verified merely because a vendor account/store is approved or has a paid tier.
- Preserve existing Enterprise and Platinum entitlements.
- Record every submission/review/decision in the audit log.
